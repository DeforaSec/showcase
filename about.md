## Approach

- **Research-first** — Every finding is documented with full technical depth, reproduction steps, and remediation guidance.
- **Responsible disclosure** — Vendors are notified and given reasonable time to patch before public publication.
- **Open by default** — Knowledge and tooling are shared to raise the baseline of security across the industry.

## Research Standards

Every submission follows the [Research Template](/research/RESEARCH_TEMPLATE.md):

- **Objective** — Scope and goals of the investigation
- **Research Process** — Methodology, tools, and environment
- **Vulnerability Discovery** — Findings with reproduction steps
- **Remediation** — Fixes, patches, and vendor response
- **Impact** — Real-world implications and risk assessment

All research is conducted under responsible disclosure practices.
